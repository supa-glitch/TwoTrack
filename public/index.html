The problem

The group behind your back goes off task.

During group work you can only stand at one table. While you help one group, the group behind your back drifts off task, and the lesson ends early with a rushed exit ticket.

How it works

Three steps, and you can see the whole room.

  1. 1

    Paste your worksheet and answer key.

    Your own activities and problems, as plain text. You see exactly what students will get before class starts.

  2. 2

    Groups work through it together on one computer.

    One problem at a time, checked right away. The next activity unlocks when they finish the one they're on.

  3. 3

    Watch every group live on your board.

    See who's done, who's stuck, and who went quiet, so you know which table needs you next.
